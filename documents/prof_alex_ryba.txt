Professor: Alex Ryba
Department: Computer Science
University: Queens College, CUNY
Courses: CS 700, CS 490
Source: RateMyProfessors.com
Review 1
CS211 with him is very theory heavy and focuses a lot on proofs and formal logic. Lectures are kind of fast and you really need discrete math background. Exams are written and not multiple choice, mostly proving stuff.

Review 2
Took CS212 and it was honestly confusing at first because he jumps into abstract concepts quickly. If you miss class you are basically lost. Office hours help but only if you already tried the problems.

Review 3
His CS111 section was okay but still more theoretical than expected. Labs were manageable but exams had tricky written questions. Grading felt fair but strict on showing steps.

Review 4
Lectures are not very engaging and can feel monotone. That said, the material is consistent with exams so you cant really skip. Homework is important for exam prep.

Review 5
CS211 with him felt like a math class more than CS. Lots of proofs and reasoning questions on exams. You need to practice a lot outside class.

Review 6
He is not bad but definitely not beginner friendly. CS111 students struggled a bit with the pace. Office hours are useful if you go prepared.

Review 7
Exams are all written and require full explanations. No multiple choice at all in my experience. You really need to understand every concept deeply.

Review 8
Lectures are dense but organized. If you rewrite notes after class you can survive CS212. Otherwise it gets overwhelming fast.

Review 9
Fair professor but definitely not an easy A. CS211 requires consistent studying every week. Cramming does not work here.

Review 10
Good if you like theoretical CS but not great if you want practical coding. CS111 felt harder than expected. Still learned a lot though.
