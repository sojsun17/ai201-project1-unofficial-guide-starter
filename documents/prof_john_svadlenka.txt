Professor: John Svadlenka
Department: Computer Science
University: Queens College, CUNY
Courses: CS 111, CS 112
Source: RateMyProfessors.com
--- Review 1 ---
This course has a very heavy workload with frequent assignments and strict deadlines. Time management is essential to survive the semester.

--- Review 2 ---
Lectures are dense and move quickly, making it easy to fall behind if you miss even a few classes. Staying consistent is critical.

--- Review 3 ---
Exams are significantly more difficult than expected based on lecture material. You need extra preparation beyond class notes.

--- Review 4 ---
The course requires a lot of independent reading and self study. Lecture alone is not enough to succeed.

--- Review 5 ---
Students often feel overwhelmed due to the volume of work. The pace of the course is demanding.

--- Review 6 ---
Grading is strict and small mistakes can significantly impact your final grade. Attention to detail is important.

--- Review 7 ---
This is not a good choice for students looking for a lighter workload. It requires serious commitment.

--- Review 8 ---
Material is important but not always clearly explained in lecture. Outside study resources are often necessary.

--- Review 9 ---
The professor expects a high level of discipline and consistency from students. Falling behind is difficult to recover from.

--- Review 10 ---
Overall one of the more demanding professors, but manageable for strong and organized students.
