Professor: John Svadlenka
Department: Computer Science
University: Queens College, CUNY
Courses: CS 111, CS 112
Source: RateMyProfessors.com
--- Review 1 ---
CS111 with him was honestly way harder than I expected for an intro class. The lectures are kind of fast and he doesn’t really slow down even when people look confused. Exams are written and significantly harder than homework problems. There is no real curve so you feel every mistake. Office hours help but only if you already understand most of the material.

--- Review 2 ---
Took CS112 and it felt like a big jump from CS111. The workload increased a lot and assignments took way longer than expected. Exams were not multiple choice and required full written explanations. Grading was very strict on small logic errors. He does not really curve so you have to earn every point.

--- Review 3 ---
He is knowledgeable but not great at explaining basics in CS111. A lot of students struggled early because the pacing is too fast. Homework is manageable but exams are on a different level. Office hours are packed before exams and still a bit confusing. You really need outside practice to do well.

--- Review 4 ---
CS112 felt like a continuation of the same difficulty issues from CS111. Lectures are dense and sometimes hard to follow without rewatching notes. Exams are very detail oriented and time pressure is real. Grading is harsh especially on written steps. You cannot rely on memorization at all.

--- Review 5 ---
The class is structured but not forgiving. CS111 started easy but quickly became overwhelming after midterm. Exams are harder than examples given in class. He expects you to fully understand every concept. No curve made the class stressful for a lot of students.

--- Review 6 ---
CS112 had a heavy workload with long assignments every week. The material itself is not impossible but the teaching style makes it harder. Exams are written and require careful reasoning. Even small mistakes lose a lot of points. Office hours are helpful but crowded.

--- Review 7 ---
Honestly CS111 was okay if you already had coding experience. If not, it felt pretty fast. The exams were way harder than labs. He is not very flexible with grading. Still, you do learn a lot if you survive the course.

--- Review 8 ---

--- Review 9 ---
