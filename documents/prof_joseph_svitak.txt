Professor: Joseph Svitak
Department: Computer Science
University: Queens College, CUNY
Courses: CS 211, CS 313
Source: RateMyProfessors.com

--- Review 1 ---
The course is well structured with clear expectations from the beginning of the semester. Students generally know what is required to succeed.

--- Review 2 ---
If you keep up with Zybooks and complete assignments on time, the class is very manageable. Falling behind is the main reason students struggle.

--- Review 3 ---
Exams are closely aligned with lecture content and homework problems. Studying practice material is usually enough preparation.

--- Review 4 ---
Lectures are a bit dry but very organized and easy to follow. The consistency helps with exam preparation.

--- Review 5 ---
Weekly assignments reinforce the material effectively and help build understanding over time. They are essential for doing well.

--- Review 6 ---
This professor is a good choice for students who prefer structure and predictability. The course does not have surprises if you stay on track.

--- Review 7 ---
Attendance is helpful because lectures clarify details that appear on exams. Missing class makes it harder to keep up.

--- Review 8 ---
Grading is fair and based on consistent performance throughout the semester. There are no unusual grading surprises.

--- Review 9 ---
The class is not easy, but it is very passable with steady effort. Cramming before exams is not enough.

--- Review 10 ---
Overall a solid introductory theory course that rewards consistency and discipline.
