Professor: Gaurish Telang
Department: Computer Science
University: Queens College, CUNY
Courses: CS 320, CS 313
Source: RateMyProfessors.com

--- Review 1 ---
Lectures are generally clear and sometimes include humor that makes the class more engaging. The professor keeps the class from feeling too dry.

--- Review 2 ---
Exam questions are very similar to the review sheets and practice problems provided before tests. Preparation material is very useful.

--- Review 3 ---
If you attend class and study consistently, doing well in this course is very achievable. The expectations are clear.

--- Review 4 ---
The difficulty level is moderate and manageable with steady effort. It is not an overwhelming course if you stay consistent.

--- Review 5 ---
Review sessions before exams are extremely helpful and often closely match exam content. Attending them is highly recommended.

--- Review 6 ---
Assignments and exams follow a predictable structure. Once you understand the format, it becomes easier to prepare.

--- Review 7 ---
The professor is fair and transparent about grading and expectations. Students generally know where they stand.

--- Review 8 ---
This is a good course for students who stay on top of material weekly. Cramming is not very effective.

--- Review 9 ---
The teaching style is solid and supports learning through repetition and practice. Exams reflect what is taught.

--- Review 10 ---
Overall a fair and manageable course with reasonable expectations.
