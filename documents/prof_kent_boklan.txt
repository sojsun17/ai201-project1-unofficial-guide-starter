Professor: Kent Boklan
Department: Computer Science
University: Queens College, CUNY
Courses: CS 370, CS 781
Source: RateMyProfessors.com
--- Review 1 ---
This is one of the most difficult theory courses in the department. Exams are extremely challenging and require deep understanding rather than memorization.

--- Review 2 ---
The workload is heavy and students need to consistently study throughout the semester. Falling behind even slightly makes the class very difficult.

--- Review 3 ---
Many students feel that the grading does not reflect the amount of effort they put in. Even strong students report struggling with exam difficulty.

--- Review 4 ---
The material is important for theoretical computer science but is presented in a very demanding way. You need strong mathematical maturity to keep up.

--- Review 5 ---
Lectures alone are not sufficient to succeed in this course. Independent study is essentially required.

--- Review 6 ---
Assignments and exams are significantly harder than most other CS classes. Time management becomes critical for survival in this course.

--- Review 7 ---
Office hours can help clarify concepts, but they do not fully bridge the difficulty gap. You must already be comfortable with proofs.

--- Review 8 ---
Some students recommend avoiding this professor unless the course is required. The stress level is consistently high.

--- Review 9 ---
The exams are long, tricky, and require creative problem solving. Expect to spend a lot of time preparing.

--- Review 10 ---
This course is best suited for students who are very confident in theoretical CS and abstract reasoning.
